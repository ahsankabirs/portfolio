About me

Student by day, builder by night.

I like the part where an idea finally works exactly the way you imagined it.

I'm Shawon, an undergraduate student of Computer Science & Engineering at Manarat International University. My journey started with curiosity — "how does this button actually work?" — and turned into a habit of building things until they do.

Most of my time goes into mobile and web development with React Native, TypeScript, and Node.js. I also enjoy the research side of CSE: I've worked on bioinformatics experiments where code meets biology — from DNA barcode identification to protein target screening with BLAST.

Right now I'm focused on sharpening my fundamentals, shipping real projects, and looking for an internship where I can learn from people who care about clean software.
